# System Requirements: Clinic Appointments

## Functional Requirements (FR)
- **R1:** Patients shall be able to register and log in to the clinic system.
- **R2:** Doctors shall be able to manage and publish their available appointment slots.
- **R3:** Patients shall be able to search for available doctors by specialty and view their schedules.
- **R4:** Patients shall be able to book an appointment, restricted to a maximum of **N = 3** active upcoming appointments at any given time.
- **R5:** Patients shall be able to cancel an upcoming appointment prior to its scheduled time.
- **R6:** Doctors shall be able to view their daily schedule of patient appointments and mark them as completed.

## Non-Functional Requirements (NFR)
- **R7:** The system shall maintain high availability, secure user data authentication, and enforce business constraint validation (such as maximum appointment limits) in real time.
